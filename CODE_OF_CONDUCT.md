# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing viewpoints,
graceful acceptance of constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for
the community.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take
appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. Report
concerns to the maintainer privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
